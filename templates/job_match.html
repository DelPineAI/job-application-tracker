How matching works

For each job, the app compares your skills with the job's requirements JSON list:

match % = skills you have ÷ skills the job requires × 100

Example: you know Python, SQL, Flask and a job needs Python, SQL, Tableau → 2 of 3 = 67%, missing Tableau. Jobs are ranked from best to worst match.
